# Business-ideas page: cheat sheet
## Content mix (4 posts a day)
Reel (reach) / Carousel (saves) / Poster card (shares) / Numbers poster (comments).
## What makes people follow
- A promise in the first 2 seconds ("Start with Rs 5,000. Earn Rs 30,000*") and a series ("Business idea #12").
- Be useful and honest: real numbers, real steps, real risks. Pages that hype "easy money" get reported and lose trust.
- Ask for the next topic in every caption ("Which business should we explain next?") and actually make the top-commented one.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Pin the best question.
## Rules
- Never promise income. Keep "approx., not guaranteed" on every post. Avoid health, finance-return or MLM-style claims.
- Only legal, licence-light ideas; mention FSSAI/GST-type needs when relevant.
- Hashtags: 5-8 relevant. Post times are random; keep 4/day for 2 weeks then check Insights for saves and shares per reach.
## Growth habits (20 min/day)
Comment usefully on 10 posts of small-business and student pages, answer DMs, reshare comments to Stories with poll stickers ("Would you start this? Yes/No").
